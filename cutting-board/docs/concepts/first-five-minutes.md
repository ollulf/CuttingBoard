# First five minutes (concept)

Round 1, 2026-10-07. Brainstorm for the opening: you start as a faceless "nobody" and a
**Builder** makes you (the Builder's identity is still open). Each opening ends on the bandits'
masks and the Mask-Monger. The page with storyboards and a comparison table is the artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/WsMSKG8jw94fSGj34u9d4A. This file is a short text copy.

## Concepts
- **A. The Workbench Foundling** (recommended spine). The Builder carves you from a log in a shed
  but can't carve a face ("faces are Whittler work"). You start in the mask-off grain view, take
  a saw and glue from the bench, and get shunned in the village (nameplate `???`, no trade). The
  Mask-Monger has no face to sell you because bandits robbed his stock. You beat a lone bandit
  on the road, wear his cut face and get waved into Hollowstump. **~5:00 choice at the mask
  rack:** return the masks (village opens), sell them to the Monger (Soul in a Bottle plus a face
  of your own), or keep the cut face (bandit kin).
- **B. Patchwork of the Raid.** After a raid, the Builder glues you together from broken villagers'
  parts, and people recognise your arm. Bandits wear the stolen villager masks. **Choice:** give
  the three named masks back, keep one as your face (you become that person), or bottle them for
  the Monger. Risk: it can turn creepy.
- **C. Born Inside Hollowstump.** The bandits kidnapped the Builder to make a recruit. Faceless,
  you're unseen, so you sneak out the back crack. **Choice:** wear the cut face and go back in
  as a recruit, trade it to the Monger for a villager face, or stay a nobody. Risk: it starts in
  the enemy camp before the player knows the rules.
- **D. The Glowing Trail.** In the grain view, masks glow as woody hearts and a trail leads east.
  The faceless Mask-Monger can see you and hires you. Turning the mask off becomes a navigation
  tool. **Choice:** bottle the hearts, return them, or keep one. Risk: too much time in a black
  screen.

## Recommendation
Use A's order (it teaches each built system once), add D's glowing hearts to the grain view, and
make one rack mask named as in B, so keeping it as your face is a real choice.

## Open questions about the Builder
- Why can't the Builder give you a face?
- Is the Builder trusted, an outcast, or a tool of the bandits?
- Does the Builder stay (as a home), vanish, or need rescue?
- Does the Builder react to what you do with the masks?
- Were there other "nobodies" before you?
