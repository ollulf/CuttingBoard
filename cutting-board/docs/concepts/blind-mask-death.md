# Blind mask death (concept)

When an NPC's mask shatters it flinches, reaches both arms straight forward, gropes a few blind steps, sinks to its knees and dies.

- Result page: https://claude.ai/artifact/WAJUxhDRXqbSdjTD7ep7PL
- Capture scene: `res://tests/visual/blind_mask_death_concept.tscn -- --variant=a` (or `b`)
- Variant A: topples forward face down, arms still stretched ahead.
- Variant B: slumps sideways onto its side.
- Procedural bone poses on the human body rig; not wired into NPC logic yet.
