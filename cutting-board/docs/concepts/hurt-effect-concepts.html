Hurt, but weirder

The shipped hurt effect is a dithered blood-red screen edge. These are eight stranger and sillier takes on it, each running live over a real frame of the game at its retro resolution (640×360) and dithered through the same 4×4 Bayer pattern as the current edge. The current effect plays alongside for comparison. A scripted demo (two light hits, three heavy, a held critical, three heals) loops until you take over: use the buttons or keys 1 light hit, 2 heavy hit, 3 hold for critical, 4 heal.

Recommendation

Make Tallow Drip the everyday hurt edge, and save the Peeking Masks for critical health. The wax is the only variant that is on theme (tallow is in the fair's name), still reads instantly as "hurt" (red, from the edges, more means worse), and scales cleanly from a couple of drips to a screen that's melting shut. It is also the cheapest step from what exists: the drips and the band are a rework of the current overlay shader plus a small per-column texture, so it keeps the 2×2-block dither and stays above the palette grade.

Layer the masks on top only below ~15% HP. Pale fair masks slowly leaning in to watch you, all tilting their heads at once, is the most memorable image on this page. Kept to critical, it stays rare enough to stay creepy.

Worth keeping on the shelf: OUCH! Stamps for a lighter mode or the fair's minigames (it is the funniest but adds text to every hit), and the Fun-house Mirror for a drunk or poisoned status rather than for damage, since constant wobble at low health is a motion-sickness risk. The Heartbeat Squash works as an add-on to any of them: scaling the retro screen's texture on the beat costs nothing.

Backdrops are captures from tests/visual/tallow_capture.tscn at 640×360. The effect logic mirrors HurtOverlay on task/hud-hurt-vignette: a flash per hit (0.35 + 2 × share of max HP, fading with a 0.45 s time constant) and a lingering, pulsing edge below 30% HP that is strongest at 10%.
