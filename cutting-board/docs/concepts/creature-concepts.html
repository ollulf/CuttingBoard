The Whittlers

A race the player meets in the second half of the game: the faceless carvers who live under the well and made every mask in the valley. Six castes, each with one odd body plan (six arms, a totem of heads, a shepherd's-crook neck, a face on the back of its head, a hand puppet that does the talking, a tail that holds a mask). Every one of them wears the mask somewhere. All six are real low-poly 3D models built in code and rendered the way the game renders: 320×240, snapped vertices, per-vertex lantern light, dither. 2 · The race

Who they are

Every mask in the valley was carved by the Whittlers. They live under the village, in root-tunnels below the well, and they have no faces: under the mask is a smooth putty egg. To a Whittler a face is a made thing, like a chair or a spoon, and you wear the one that suits the job. Long ago they traded faces to the villagers for tallow, the candle-fat that keeps the tunnels lit. That old bargain is why every villager wears the same pale mask and why nobody in the village takes it off.

The bandits wear “cut faces”: Whittler masks that were stolen or badly copied, burnt dark and bolted with antlers. The Whittlers hate this the way a carpenter hates a wobbly chair. They will help anyone who brings a cut face back, and the Warden collects them.

Why later in the game: the Whittlers only come up on nights when the moon's pupil closes. The first meeting is underground, when the player climbs down the well in the mid-game. After that, “blind moon” nights bring them up to trade in the square. When a ragdoll's mask pops off on death, a Whittler will sometimes come and pick it up.

The mask, and how it relates to ours

All three masks are the same mask: the same flat quad and the same mask.gdshader, with different material values. The Whittler mask is the original: raw birch, eyes lit from inside with moonlight. The villager mask is a Whittler mask sanded pale and sold with the light taken out of the eyes. The bandit mask is a stolen one, burnt dark and given antlers, with flame in the eye slits. No new shader is needed, only one new material, whittler_mask.tres.

Shared traits (every caste)

  • Faceless egg head. Smooth putty, no features. The mask gives it a face.
  • More than two arms. Two to six, depending on the job. Spare hands always hold a tool.
  • Backward knees. Bird-like digitigrade legs with a long ankle, so they bob when they walk.
  • Mauve candle-putty skin with seams, like a stop-motion puppet that has been mended.
  • Wood-shaving curls instead of hair. Sackcloth, leather aprons, brass rings and rope.
  • Moonlight in the eyes. A cool moon-yellow glow, the board's light-colour rule for “strange”: not trade (amber) and not bandit (flame).

FRIENDLY EVIL FUNNY WEIRD

One race, four moods

Round 1 spread fourteen creatures over the four moods. Here a single race has to cover all of them, so the mood comes from the job. Workers and children are friendly and funny. The Elder is friendly but strange. The Warden is a stern rule-keeper, and Backwards Jack is the race's thief and liar. Nobody is cruel. The evil ones are pompous or sneaky, and they back off when you stand up to them.

Not too creepy

No teeth, wounds, eyes in wrong places or twitchy motion. Extra limbs always hold something, so they read as busy hands, not as a monster. Faces are blank putty, never flesh. When the mask is off-face (on the chest, the tail, the back of the head), it is still the thing you look at, because it's the brightest object in the frame.

5 · Building them in Godot

Today's human_body is a 12-bone skin (Hips, Spine, Chest, Head, and upper arm, forearm, thigh and shin on each side) with the mask as a flat quad on the head. Every Whittler wears the mask through a BoneAttachment3D on whichever bone carries it, so mask_scene keeps working. Ragdolls get one physical bone per new bone.
